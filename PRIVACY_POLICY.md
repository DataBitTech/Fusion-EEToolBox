# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026.04.13**

This Privacy Policy describes how DataBitTech AS ("we", "us", or "our") handles data in relation to the *Electronics Extended Toolbox* add-on ("App").

## 1. Overview

The App is designed with a privacy-first approach. It does **not collect, transmit, or share personal data**.

All data generated by the App is stored **locally on the user’s device** and remains under the user’s control.

## 2. Data Collection

The App may store limited usage-related data locally, including:

* User-selected options and preferences
* Configuration settings
* Basic usage state necessary for functionality

This data:

* Is **not personal data**
* Is **not transmitted externally**

## 3. Data Storage

All data is:

* Stored locally on the user’s device
* Accessible only by the user and the App
* Not accessible to DataBitTech AS

We do not operate servers or remote services for this App.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not**:

* Collect user data
* Sell or share data
* Transmit data to third parties
* Use analytics, tracking, or telemetry services

## 5. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally:

* Users are responsible for securing their own systems and files
* The App does not implement remote security mechanisms or data transmission safeguards

## 6. User Control

Users have full control over their data:

* Data can be modified or deleted at any time by removing the App’s local files
* Uninstalling the App may remove associated data, depending on the system configuration

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.
